# Technical Rescue — Written Test Prep

Study site for the Phoenix Fire Department written test based on SOPs, Volume 2, MPs 204.08–204.13 (Revised 01/2026).

- **100-Question Mock Test** — full-length mock, mixed across all six MPs, like the real test
- **Mix All** — 25 / 50 / 100 questions across all six MPs
- **Per-MP drills** — 18 questions each, 10 or 18 at a time

MPs covered: 204.08 Confined Space Rescue · 204.09 Trench Rescue · 204.10 Mountain Rescue · 204.11 Water Rescue · 204.12 Structural Collapse Rescue · 204.13 Tree Rescue

108 multiple-choice questions total. Every question cites the MP section it comes from. Questions are a study aid written from the SOPs — not an official department test. When in doubt, the SOP itself is the answer.
